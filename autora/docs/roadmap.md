# Autora — Future Roadmap

## Phase 1: Auto Spare Parts Marketplace
- Product listings with categories, photos, and specifications
- Shopping cart and order management
- Seller dashboard for inventory management
- Integration with delivery services
- Price comparison across sellers

## Phase 2: Car Wash Booking Integration
- Partner with local car wash businesses
- Schedule and manage car wash appointments
- Different wash packages (basic, premium, detailing)
- Loyalty discounts for regular customers
- Real-time slot availability

## Phase 3: Nationwide Coverage Expansion
- Expand from Tashkent to all major cities in Uzbekistan
- Region-specific service pricing
- Local service provider onboarding campaigns
- Multi-language support (Uzbek, Russian, English)
- Regional marketing and partnerships

## Phase 4: Driver Loyalty & Rewards Program
- Points system for completed bookings and reviews
- Tier-based rewards (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum)
- Partner discounts at fuel stations, parking, and restaurants
- Referral bonuses for inviting friends
- Monthly challenges and achievements

## Phase 5: Insurance Integration
- Partner with Uzbekistan insurance companies
- In-app insurance quotes and policy purchase
- Accident reporting with photo documentation
- Claims tracking and management
- Roadside assistance bundled with insurance

## Phase 6: AI-Based Diagnostics Recommendations
- ML model trained on vehicle symptoms and repair data
- Users describe symptoms → AI suggests likely issues
- Recommended service providers based on the diagnosis
- Cost estimates for common repairs
- Preventive maintenance reminders based on vehicle age/mileage

## Phase 7: Fleet Management (B2B)
- Dashboard for taxi companies and logistics firms
- Bulk booking and maintenance scheduling
- Fleet-wide analytics (costs, downtime, mileage)
- Driver management and assignment
- Automated maintenance alerts

## Phase 8: Electric Vehicle Support
- EV charging station finder and navigation
- Battery health monitoring integration
- EV-specialized service providers
- Charging cost comparison
- Range estimation tools
